# Upgrade 2026-11-16: Church Street Runner

Branch `upgrade-2026-11-16`. Plan: `ADVISOR.md`. Brief from Stephen: "too hard
and repetitive... I want people to keep coming back." The fix is to make
distance change the run, make every hit feel fair, and clear the screen.

Not pushed and not deployed. `docs/` is rebuilt and committed, so merging this
branch to main is what would ship it.

## What changed (in plan order)

1. **Blocks.** The run is now 420 m city blocks. Each block starts at a cross
   street with 30 m of coins and no obstacles, then a "BLOCK 3 · DELIVERY HOUR
   +50" card. Six themes change the hazard models, the scenery bias and the
   time of day (morning, noon, afternoon, golden, sunset, night, with
   lamps and windows glowing at night). After block 6 the street loops as
   "Day 2". One speed curve replaces Easy/Hard.
2. **Authored patterns.** 20 patterns in 4 tiers replace the random rows, plus
   Day 2 pairs. Tier 3 (block 3) adds rows that force a jump (`SJS`, `JJJ`)
   or a slide (`SGS`, `GGG`). Coins trace the safe route: a line through the
   open lane, an arc over a low prop, a low line under a gate.
3. **Fair hits.** Swipes fire on touchmove (24 px) and re-anchor, so you can
   chain swipes without lifting. The jump is higher, a swipe up just before
   landing is buffered, the slide is longer and hitboxes are tighter. Cars
   wait at the crosswalk and never hit you. You get 3 lives, shown as pips.
   The slide gate is now safety orange with white stripes, and the sawhorse
   that used to be orange is now yellow, so orange always means "duck".
   Obstacles disappear once they are behind you, so passed walkers no longer
   fill the bottom of the screen.
4. **Clear view.** All the over-street news banners are gone; headlines now run
   in the shared ticker (`ticker.js`), which hides during play and comes back on
   the menus. The portrait camera is lower and tighter. The runner has a cream
   ground ring and a slight glow (stronger at night). The sidewalks are darker
   and decor sits further out. There are 4 ambient walkers instead of 10. The
   HUD is a bare score, lives pips and the block name; the coins and a small
   mute button sit top right; a power timer bar shows only while a power-up is
   active.
5. **Today on Church Street.** The America/New_York date picks a twist (Dog
   Day, Creemee Day, Maple Rush, Festival Day, Night Owl) and the order of the
   middle blocks. Twists rotate in shuffled five-day cycles, so the same one
   doesn't come up two days running. A daily streak and today's best show on
   the title.
6. **Unlockable runners.** The Busker (reach block 3), the Mail Carrier (block
   5), the Chef (500 lifetime coins) and the Night Owl (finish block 6). Locked
   chips show the requirement when tapped. The game-over screen shows one "Next
   runner" goal with a progress bar. A new runner gets a callout during the
   run and a "NEW RUNNER" card at game over.
7. **Personal-best line.** Your best distance is chalked across the street,
   with a pole and a "BEST 1,255" sign on each grey strip so you can see it
   coming. Crossing it fires "NEW BEST!", the fanfare and confetti mid-run. A
   fainter TODAY line appears when today's best is well short of your all-time
   best.
8. **Title and game over: one decision each.** Title: logo, today's twist,
   best and streak, one scrolling row of runners, and a big RUN button. Game
   over: the result, score, block/coins/best, one goal, and RUN AGAIN at 72% of
   the screen height. The leaderboard folds into one row ("You're #4 in
   November") that opens a bottom sheet with the board, the tabs and the name
   form. "Press Space" is hidden on touch screens. Tap anywhere to retry after
   700 ms.
9. **Polish.** The game pauses when the tab is hidden and resumes with a 3-2-1
   countdown. The canvas is resized only on orientation or width changes, not
   when Safari's toolbar moves. There is a 90 ms hit-stop and vibration on
   hits and close calls. Coin chains rise in pitch. Off-screen street chunks
   are built in idle time, so RUN is ready sooner. The first run teaches by
   doing: a low prop, a walker and a gate, each with a ghost arrow ("SWIPE
   UP / SIDEWAYS / DOWN"). Those three bump you without costing a life.
   `README.md` is new.

## Acceptance tests (ADVISOR.md)

All 14 are covered by `/tmp/upgrade-church-street-runner/new/accept.js` (30
checks, 30 pass) plus the bot and screenshot scripts in the same folder. The
leaderboard checks intercept Supabase, so no test score reached the real board.

- Build OK. `docs/index.html` points at the new bundle and the old one is gone. No console errors.
- Hooks: `__csr` has `block`, `blockName`, `patternLog`, `setDate`, and keeps the old keys. `__T` has `start`, `step(ms)`, `state()` and `setDate`.
- The first coin comes at 1.4 s and the first obstacle reaches you at 4.3 s. Speed is 12.0 m/s at 31 s.
- The idle player dies at 27-51 s. The lane-only bot dies in block 3. The full-move bot reaches block 4 in 5 of 5 runs.
- Screenshots at 5, 60 and 120 s show different skies and different hazards. A 3-block run logs 12 or more distinct patterns.
- Input: a swipe registers before the finger lifts, two swipes in one touch give two moves, a swipe that starts on the mute button is ignored, and two fingers don't break lanes.
- Daily: the same date gives the same plan, the next date changes it, the streak goes 1 -> 2 and resets after a gap, and the title shows the streak from 2.
- Old saves keep `csr-best`, `csr-char` and the `btown-player-*` keys.
- `submit_score` posts `p_game: 'church-street-runner'`. The collapsed row shows your rank and the sheet renders the board.
- The ticker is hidden while running and shows on the title and game-over screens.
- One tap retries after the grace delay.
- The Chef unlocks at 500 coins and is still selectable after a reload.

## Decisions and deviations

- **Time of day follows block position, not theme.** The daily order shuffles
  the themes, so tying the sky to a theme would make it jump from morning to
  night and back. Instead the sky always runs morning -> night (Night Owl runs
  it backwards), and Night Market is always block 6 except on Night Owl days.
- **Gentle opening.** For the first 200 m the centre lane is left clear (side
  singles, pairs, hop lines and staggers only). This is what keeps the idle
  player alive past 20 s and gives new players a calm first wave.
- **Festival is a reward block:** one tier easier, twice the coin lines, and a
  guaranteed creemee.
- **Close calls** now count only a late sidestep or a jump that barely clears.
  Before, every hop over a prop fired "CLOSE ONE!", so the callout was
  constant noise.
- **Coins** were cut back after the first pass. Route coins on open rows went
  from 3 to 2, gap lines are now 30% likely and at most 5 coins. The first pass
  gave ~3.5 coins/s, which would have unlocked the Chef in one run.
- **The HUD text shadow** lost its 12 px blur. That blur alone dropped 4x
  throttled play from 59 to 53 fps.

## Known issues / notes for Stephen

- **Leaderboard:** scores per run are higher now (longer runs, a +50 bonus per
  block, more coins), so November's monthly board will mix old and new runs.
  The game id and RPC calls are unchanged.
- The lane-only bot reached block 4 once in 18 runs (Maple Rush day). Lane
  changes alone almost never get past block 3, but it's not a hard wall.
- Night blocks are darker. Dark-shirted runners (Sam) rely on the ground ring
  and the night glow to stand out.
- The canvas is CSS-stretched between resizes. Safari toolbar changes stretch
  the picture by a few percent instead of re-laying it out. This hasn't been
  checked on a real iPhone.
- `csr-mode` and `csr-news` are no longer read. They are left in place, as asked.
- The ticker's auto-dodge checks about once a second, so after the leaderboard
  sheet closes the ticker can take a moment to come back.
